## 1. Executive summary

The nightly retention prune never honored the `decision_history_prune_max_rows_per_night`
setting (default 200000). `SQLiteDB.prune_decision_history()` still carried a hidden
`max_batches=10` limit, and the bot's nightly path never overrode it. With the default
batch size of 5000 the prune therefore stopped after 50000 rows — 25% of the configured
cap.

**Fix:** the batch count is now DERIVED from the max-rows setting
(`ceil(max_rows_per_night / batch_size)`) both in the DB layer (default when
`max_batches` is not passed) and explicitly in the bot's nightly path. Batch size 5000
and the short-transaction behavior are unchanged. The max-rows setting is now the ONLY
nightly limit.

## 2. Files changed

- `src/database/sqlite_db.py` — `prune_decision_history(max_batches=None)`; the batch
  budget is derived from `max_rows_per_night` unless a caller passes an explicit
  `max_batches` (still honored, backwards compatible). The derived value is reported in
  the summary as `max_batches`.
- `src/core/smart_bot.py` — `run_decision_history_retention_prune()` computes
  `max_batches = ceil(max_rows / batch_size)` from the bot's own settings and passes it
  together with `max_rows_per_night`.
- `tests/test_db_growth_fix_5.py` (NEW, 6 tests) — direct prune and the bot's nightly
  path, both for "more than 10 batches required" and "stops exactly at the limit",
  plus the derived-budget and explicit-`max_batches` contracts.
- `MAP.md` — documents that the max-rows setting is the only nightly limit.

No existing test was modified, weakened or skipped.
