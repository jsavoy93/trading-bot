## 1. Task

STRAT-022: pre-registered backtest of a short-term dip-buy rule
(close > SMA200 AND RSI(2) < threshold; exit close>SMA5 / ATR stop / time stop)
on a liquid US equity universe, dev 2021–2024, locked holdout 2025–2026,
vs a matched random-entry baseline with identical exits and costs.

Prior-work dedupe: STRAT-008/009/010/011 studied the production signal
(RSI<35 & SMA10>SMA30) on a 60-day window — different rule/window, not repeated.
STRAT-012's holdout discipline followed. STRAT-021 (monthly MOM/LVOL/GP
sub-sleeves) is unrelated cadence.

## 2. Data & universe

- Daily bars, Alpaca paper data API (`data.alpaca.markets/v2/stocks/bars`),
  2020-06-01 → 2026-10-29, `adjustment=raw`. 379/380 symbols OK
  (SONC returned no bars; expected — delisted). 380 Alpaca calls, logged in
  `experiments/strat_022/_download_manifest.json`.
- Universe: deduped STRAT-006R curated liquid list (~380 names).
- Point-in-time liquidity filter (no lookahead): 20d avg volume ≥ 1M shares AND
  close ≥ $5 at signal day — identical for strategy and baseline.
- **Survivorship bias — explicit caveat**: pool built from currently-known
  tickers → delisted names missing; bias is UPWARD on all results. Direction
  stated; not quantified (no delisting feed available; consistent with the
  STRAT-020 Phase 5 approach of documenting coverage gaps).

## 3. Rule (exactly as preregistered)

Entry (on close t): `close > SMA200` AND Wilder `RSI(2) < ENTRY_RSI` AND
liquidity filter. Execution at NEXT day's open (causal). One position per
symbol; new signals ignored while in a trade (anti-overlap).
Exits (first hit, checked on close, exit at that close — no intraday data):
`close > SMA5`, `close ≤ entry_open − ATR_MULT×ATR14(signal day)`, or time stop.
Grid (pre-declared, all 8 run, no others): ENTRY_RSI {10,5} × ATR_MULT {2,3} ×
TIME_STOP {5,10} = C1..C8. Costs 5 bps and 10 bps per side, both reported.
Baseline: matched-count random entries per symbol (excluded all signal days),
same exits/stops/costs, seed 20221110, single draw.

aggregation is noisy and per-trade metrics are the preregistered primary.

## 7. Caveats (all pre-declared in prereg §12, plus findings)

1. Survivorship bias — upward on all results (§2).
2. Stops checked on close only; no intraday data; gaps priced at worse close.
3. `adjustment=raw` — split artifacts possible; $5 floor limits but does not
   eliminate impact. Symbols with 2021–2026 splits may show false dips/stops.
4. Baseline is a single seeded draw; baseline uncertainty understated.
5. Baseline N > strat N (6044 vs 3485 holdout; ~13k vs ~7.5k dev): matched
   ENTRY-COUNT was declared (pre-overlap), and clustered strategy signals hit
   the anti-overlap rule more than spread-out random entries. Identical rule
   for both sides; documented, not tuned.
6. Holdout is ~22 months, one regime; low power. Equity-level annSharpe/maxDD
   assume equal-weight averaging across all concurrent signals — not a
   capital-constrained portfolio.
7. SONC excluded (no bars — survivorship caveat in action).

## 8. Verdict

Dev: 4/8 configs pass the frozen bar; selected C4 (RSI(2)<10, 2x→3.0 ATR mult,
10-day time stop). Holdout: PASS the frozen bar (positive net mean, t=3.09,
above matched baseline) but with a narrowed edge over baseline and baseline
itself strongly positive in a bull window. **Conditional accept: the rule has
positive net expectancy after costs and beats random entries, but the alpha
vs baseline is modest (~10 bps/trade holdout) and regime-dependent.** Any
production consideration would require Josh's explicit separate approval;
nothing here changes runtime code.
