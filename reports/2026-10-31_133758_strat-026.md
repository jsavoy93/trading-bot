# STRAT-026 — Pro-Swing Strategy Research (paper only)

## Executive summary
Tested four pro-swing strategy families (RSI(2) dip + regime filter with pro
exits; 20-day volume breakout + regime + pro exits; PEAD gap+volume proxy;
weekly medium-term momentum 1/2/4w) on the frozen STRAT-025 universe
(672 daily CSVs, dev 2021-2024, holdout 2025-01-01..2026-10-28, 10 bps/side
costs, 1000-draw random-entry baseline with identical exits). Only
**rsi2_regime** cleared the pre-declared dev gate (t vs random baseline
2.74 >= 2). Its one-shot holdout edge was positive (+0.579% vs baseline
+0.373% per trade, strat t=5.19) but t_vs_base = 1.84 — just under the bar:
promising, not confirmed. Momentum was negative at all horizons; volume
breakout added nothing; PEAD proxy did not qualify (t 1.36).
